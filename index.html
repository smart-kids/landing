ShulePlus Features

Timely Messaging to Parents

With ShulePlus, parents can get notified from wherever they are when their children are dropped off or picked up by the school driver.

Feedback From Parents

Parents can give back feedback via the app for any queries or complains for immediate action.

24 Hour Support

There is a large team on board to ensure smooth running of all operations and easy contact whenever needed.

How to use

1

Registration

After downloading the app, register your details and enter the verification code sent to your phone.
2

Free Trial

A free trial is given after registration where you are able to use the app for a given period of time before proceeding to payment.
3

Onboarding and training of drivers

Details of school drivers are filled to the system and drivers get training on how to use the app and send messages to parents on pickup and drop-off of students.

Why Use ShulePlus?

Students, parents, and teachers can experience the following benefits from the system.
Parent

Get notified immediately when your child is dropped off or picked up for school from wherever you are.

Students

Assured of safety since parents are are when they are picked up and dropped off from school.

Teachers

Easy and manageable system for following up with the driver on students arrival at school and drop off home.
